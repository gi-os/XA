Privacy policy · October 2026

XA privacy

XA collects nothing. It has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads.

Pictures and videos

XA saves what you shoot to your Photos library, in an XA album. It doesn't upload anything. The roll reads that album to show you your pictures.

Camera and microphone

XA uses the camera while it's open, or while its Lock Screen camera is open, to show the viewfinder and take pictures. It uses the microphone only while you record a video.

QR codes

XA reads QR codes on your iPhone. Tapping one opens its link in your browser, or copies plain text. XA doesn't keep a list of the codes it reads.
