XA

A camera for iPhone that's fun to use, and serious when you want it to be. What you see in the viewfinder is what you get in the photo.

How it works

  • DIGI: a 2003 point-and-shoot with film sims, looks, shapes and date backs. Long-press a sim to edit it.
  • FILM: pick a stock and a format, add flash, a light leak or a mist filter, and time the print.
  • PRO: the iPhone at full quality, with lens buttons, a live histogram and manual controls.
  • VIDEO: twelve tapes, from a clean picture to camcorder, home movie, security camera, slit-scan and datamosh.
  • BOOTH: four shots on a photo-sticker sheet.
  • Roll: pull down on the viewfinder to see your pictures. Flick up to go back.
  • Camera Control: slide through sims and looks, and click to shoot. The volume buttons shoot too.

Lock Screen camera

XA has a Lock Screen camera and a Control Center control. On iPhone 16 and later, open Settings › Camera › Camera Control › Launch Camera and pick XA.

Your pictures

XA saves every picture and video to Photos, in an XA album.
