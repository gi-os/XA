XA is a camera app. Test it on an iPhone. The simulator has no camera.

There is no account and no sign-in. XA has no server, analytics or ads. Photos and videos stay on the iPhone.

To try it:
1. Allow Camera, Microphone and Photos when XA asks. The microphone records sound for videos.
2. Pick DIGI, FILM, PRO, VIDEO or BOOTH in the mode row under the viewfinder.
3. Tap the shutter or press a volume button to take a picture. XA saves it to Photos, in an XA album.
4. Tap the square roll button left of the shutter (or swipe up from the shutter row) to open the roll. Drag the roll down to close it.

Lock Screen camera: XA includes a Lock Screen camera extension and a Control Center control. On iPhone 16 and later, Settings > Camera > Camera Control > Launch Camera can open XA.

QR codes: in DIGI, FILM or PRO, point the camera at a QR code. Tap the chip to open the link in the browser.

Tips: Customize (the sliders button) > Support XA offers three optional tips (consumable in-app purchases com.gios.xa.tip.small, .medium, .large). They unlock nothing: every feature is free for everyone.
