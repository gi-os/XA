XA is a camera app. Test it on an iPhone. The simulator has no camera.

There is no account and no sign-in. XA has no server, analytics or ads. Photos and videos stay on the iPhone. XA is free and has no in-app purchases or subscription.

To try it:
1. Open XA and allow Camera. XA asks for Photos when you take the first picture: choose Allow Full Access, so XA can keep its XA album. XA asks for the microphone the first time you open VIDEO. The microphone is on while VIDEO is on screen, and XA keeps sound only while you record.
2. XA has five modes: DIGI, FILM, PRO, VIDEO and BOOTH. The mode row is at the bottom of the screen, under the shutter. XA opens in DIGI, and VIDEO and BOOTH are off-screen to the right at first. Swipe the mode row sideways (swiping the controls just above it works too), or tap a mode name that is showing, to reach every mode.
3. Tap the shutter or press a volume button to take a picture. XA saves it to Photos, in an XA album. In VIDEO the shutter starts and stops a recording. BOOTH uses the front camera and takes four shots on a countdown.
4. Tap the square roll button left of the shutter (or swipe up from the shutter row) to open the roll. Drag the roll down to close it.

Lock Screen camera and Control Center control:
1. Open XA once and allow Camera.
2. Add the XA control. For Control Center: swipe down from the top right corner, tap + at the top left, tap Add a Control, then choose XA. For the Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, tap Lock Screen, tap the minus on a control at the bottom, tap the plus that appears, choose XA, then tap Done.
3. Lock the iPhone and wake it. Tap the XA control on the Lock Screen, or swipe down from the top right corner and tap XA in Control Center. The XA camera opens over the Lock Screen. If the iPhone is unlocked, the same control opens the XA app.
4. The Lock Screen camera offers DIGI, FILM and PRO. VIDEO and BOOTH are in the app only. Its roll button asks for Face ID or the passcode, then opens XA.
5. Take a picture. XA puts it in Photos, in the XA album, right away or the next time XA opens.

Camera Control: on an iPhone with Camera Control, go to Settings > Camera > Camera Control > Launch Camera > XA. The button then opens XA. A light press locks focus and a full press takes the picture. Other iPhones have no Camera Control: use the steps above.

QR codes: in DIGI, FILM or PRO, point the camera at a QR code. A chip appears. Tap it to open the link in the matching app: web links in the browser, and mail, phone, Messages, Maps and similar links in their own apps. A QR code with plain text copies the text instead.
