XA is a camera that's fun to use, and serious when you want it to be. What you see in the viewfinder is what you get in the photo.

Five cameras
• DIGI is a 2003 point-and-shoot. Pick 1, 2, 3 or 5 megapixels and get its low-end processing on purpose: clipped highlights, a sharpening halo, color noise and a JPEG squeezed hard.
• FILM loads a roll. Pick a stock and a format, add flash, a light leak or a mist filter, then time the print the way a lab does.
• PRO is the iPhone at full quality. It saves the biggest photo the sensor makes as HEIF or JPEG, with lens buttons, a live histogram and manual white balance, exposure, shutter, ISO and focus.
• VIDEO has twelve tapes, from a clean picture to camcorder, home movie, security camera, slit-scan and datamosh.
• BOOTH is a photo-sticker booth. Four shots, big eyes and a sticker sheet.

Make it yours
• Film sims you can edit, down to color, tone curve, split tone, grain, halation and vignette. Save your own.
• Looks: Clean, Grain, Sixteen, Pocket, Pocket Color, 1-Bit, Press, Heat and Booth.
• Shapes: Capsule, Porthole, Window, Crush, Nova and four instant prints.
• Date backs: seven-segment quartz, dot matrix, camcorder, LCD, red ink stamp, marker on tape and film edge print.

Always ready
• A Lock Screen camera and a Control Center control. On iPhone 16 and later, set XA as the Camera Control button's camera in Settings.
• The Camera Control button slides through sims and looks. Click it or press a volume button to shoot.
• Point at a QR code in DIGI, FILM or PRO and tap the chip to open the link.

Your roll
XA saves to Photos, in an XA album. Pull down on the viewfinder to see your roll.

No account, no ads, no tracking. XA is free and open source.
