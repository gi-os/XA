Shoot like it's 2003. Blown-out party flash, crunchy CCD color and an orange date in the corner, plus film you load and print, twelve video tapes and a photo-sticker booth.
Every camera, stock, sim, look, shape, date back and tape is included. No subscription, no ads, no account. Optional tips unlock nothing.

Five cameras in one app: DIGI, FILM, VIDEO, BOOTH and PRO.

DIGI
• Pick 1, 2, 3, 5, 8 or 12 megapixels.
• Cheap processing, the good kind: clipped highlights, a sharpening halo, color noise and a hard JPEG crunch.
• CCD color: deep cyan skies and reds a little too hot.
• Pop the flash for the party look: hot faces and warm whites.
• Bright lights smear a line down the frame, like an old CCD.
• Turn each flaw up, down or off.
• The screen is a live digicam display, battery icon and all.

FILM
• Eleven stocks modeled on published datasheets: eight color, from a fine-grain 100 to a tungsten 500T and a 1600, and three black-and-white films up to 3200.
• Grain and halation are sized in microns on the negative, so the format changes them: 110 is tiny and gritty, 120 big and smooth.
• Compose in a pocket rangefinder's bright frame, with its meter needle and focusing patch.
• In front of the film: a cheap lens, flash falloff, light leaks and a mist filter. The stock colors them all.
• Swipe a stock up to push it a stop, down to pull it.
• In the lab: minilab auto-correction, print warmth and tint, and preflash.
• Drum-scanned by default at about 5600 dpi, so zooming in shows grain, not pixels. Saved as HEIC.
• On iPhones that shoot RAW, FILM can develop from RAW and skip the iPhone's own processing.

VIDEO: TWELVE TAPES
• VHS: soft color, scanlines, a tracking band and the PLAY display.
• An 8mm home movie that flickers and weaves.
• CCTV: green-gray security footage with the clock burned in.
• Low-res pocket tapes: green pixels, color pixels and a wide 480×240 band.
• Slit-scan, trails, motion, stop motion and datamosh for the weird stuff, and a clean tape for plain video.

BOOTH
• Four shots on a countdown, with the front camera.
• Big eyes and glowing skin: Glow, Doll or Alien.
• A new backdrop and neon-pen doodles for every shot: stars, hearts, roses, cat ears.
• Print it as a sticker sheet, a grid or a strip.

PRO
• The biggest photo the sensor makes, saved as HEIF or JPEG.
• Lens buttons, a live histogram and dials for white balance, exposure, shutter, ISO and focus.
• Auto, shutter priority, ISO priority or fully manual.

MAKE IT YOURS
• In DIGI, open any film sim in the editor: color, tone curve, split tone, grain, halation and vignette. Save your own.
• Looks: Clean, Grain, Sixteen, Pocket, Pocket Color, 1-Bit, Press, Heat and Booth.
• Shapes: Capsule, Porthole, Window, Crush, Nova and four instant prints.
• Eight date backs: seven-segment quartz, dot matrix, camcorder, LCD, red-pixel digicam, red ink stamp, marker on tape and film edge print.
• Stack a sim, a look, a shape and a date back.

ALWAYS READY
• A Lock Screen camera and a Control Center control.
• On an iPhone with Camera Control, make XA the button's camera in Settings.
• Camera Control slides through sims and looks in DIGI, stocks in FILM, tapes in VIDEO, and exposure and zoom in PRO. Click it to shoot.
• The volume buttons shoot too.
• Point at a QR code in DIGI, FILM or PRO and tap to open the link.

YOUR PHOTOS
• Every picture and video goes to Photos, in an XA album.
• With iCloud Photos, they're backed up with the rest of your library.
• Swipe up under the viewfinder, or tap the roll button, for a contact sheet of your roll.
• No watermark.

NOTHING COLLECTED
• No tracking: no server, no analytics, no data collected.
• Optional tips, at the end of Customize, help keep XA going.
• XA is open source under the GPLv3. Anyone can read the code and check what it does.
• Film modeling powered by spektrafilm, Andrea Volpato's open film simulation.

GOOD TO KNOW
• iPhone only, iOS 18 or later.
• The app's interface is in English.
