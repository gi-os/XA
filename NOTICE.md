## spektrafilm

Film modeling in XA is powered by, and inspired by, [spektrafilm](https://github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm)
by Andrea Volpato: a spectral simulation of analog photography from published film datasheets.
spektrafilm code is GPL version 3. Its stock profiles and the LUTs it generates are CC BY-SA 4.0.
Work in `tools/filmsim` uses spektrafilm and stays under those licenses.
